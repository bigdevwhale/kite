# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

- Tugs: a few times a day, at moments you can't guess, a soft buzz and "Where are you?". Spread evenly across the hours you pick; "Here" answers from the notification itself.
- Where the mind was: here, in the past, in the future, in cares.
- Reeling in: hold the spool while you find one thing you see, one sound and one feeling; the kite comes down into the grass in three turns.
- "I drifted off": a button and a home-screen widget to reel in without waiting for a tug.
- The day's sky: one kite per tug, coloured by where the mind was, reeled in or let go; one line in words and the week's skies.
- Quiet for an hour, spool clicks, a sky that follows the clock. English and Russian. No account, no internet.
